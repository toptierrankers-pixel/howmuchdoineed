Why HowMuchDoINeed

Every calculator here does one thing and does it fast: enter your measurements, read the answer. No accounts, no walls of ads over the tool, no guessing at the math. Each one also shows the formula and a worked example, so you can check the number yourself or run it by hand on site.

Estimates include a waste factor because real jobs aren't tidy: ground isn't level, material settles, and some is always lost in handling. The goal is a number you can order from with confidence. Always confirm density and coverage with your supplier for the exact product you're buying.
