01How to calculate topsoil

Topsoil is bought by volume for bulk loads and by the bag for small jobs. Start with length × width × depth, convert depth to feet, then split into cubic yards, tons, or bags.

cubic feet = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 cubic yards = cubic feet ÷ 27 tons ≈ cubic yards × 1.1 40 lb bags = cubic feet ÷ 0.75

The 1.1 tons per cubic yard is a working average for screened topsoil. Damp or clay-heavy soil weighs more; a dry, peaty mix weighs less. A 40 lb bag holds roughly 0.75 cubic feet.

02Worked example

A 12 ft × 12 ft lawn area, 2 inches deep

  1. Volume in cubic feet: 12 × 12 × (2 ÷ 12) = 24 ft³
  2. Convert to cubic yards: 24 ÷ 27 = 0.89 yd³
  3. Convert to tons: 0.89 × 1.1 = 0.98 tons
  4. In 40 lb bags: 24 ÷ 0.75 = 32 bags
Order about 1 cubic yard (≈ 1 ton), cheaper than 32 bags for this size.

04Common questions

How many tons is a cubic yard of topsoil?

Around 1.1 tons for screened topsoil, but moisture swings it a lot. Wet or clay-rich soil can hit 1.3 tons or more. Ask your supplier what their product weighs.

How deep should topsoil be for a new lawn?

Two to three inches of quality topsoil gives grass seed enough to root into. For raised beds and vegetables, go much deeper: six inches at minimum, more for root crops.

How many 40 lb bags in a cubic yard?

About 36 bags, since each holds roughly 0.75 cubic feet. That's why bulk delivery almost always wins for anything larger than a small patch.

Does topsoil settle?

Yes, especially fresh screened soil. Add a little extra depth and water it in, or expect the finished level to drop slightly over the first few weeks.
